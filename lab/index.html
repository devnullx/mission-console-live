LELP-1 · FREE-FLYING, RECOVERABLE

A laboratory in orbit for life science in microgravity

LELP-1 carries 32 sealed experiment bays for 2 to 14 days at 550 km, runs them automatically, and brings the whole lab home under an inflatable heat shield. Each customer signs their own commands. This catalog shows 15 experiments LELP-1 is designed to fly, what earlier missions found, and exactly what each one needs from the satellite.

Flown in the twin: RadSenRegen-1

RadSenRegen's WJ-MSCs vs Muse cells, with and without exosomes (7 days, module 17) is the first customer experiment flown end to end in Space Philic's mission twin: cryopreserved launch, automated thaw at 37 °C, exosome dosing, imaging every 4 h, dosimetry, preservation, return and courier handover. How a flight works

  1. L − 24 hLate load

    cells and samples go into their bays frozen or dormant; the lab is closed

  2. T + 0Launch on RUPAK

    the reusable booster flies back; the upper stage carries the lab to orbit

  3. 0 h 47 minOrbit

    550 km dawn-dusk sun-synchronous, trimmed by the upper stage

  4. 1 h 13 minYour start

    you sign the start; the bay thaws and runs your protocol on its own

  5. days 1 to 14Operations

    media changes, dosing, imaging, dosimetry; daily data through ground stations in India and Europe

  6. 7 d 2 h 14 minPreserve and pack

    samples fixed, lysed or chilled; every bay latched for entry

  7. 7 d 3 h 20 minHome

    inflatable heat shield, parachute, splashdown at 7.9 m/s

  8. 7 d 3 h 42 minYour courier

    bay handed over on the recovery ship, 21 min after splashdown

Times from a run of the mission twin (lab operations last as long as the longest protocol on board; this run: 7.1 days).

Where LELP-1 sits among earlier platforms

Most results on the experiment pages come from the ISS and from free-flyers and capsules like these. Two 2025 capsules show how hard the return is: one splashed down far from its ships, one was lost before splashdown. LELP-1's niche: many independent customers per flight, a launch from India, and the whole lab back on a ship next to the splashdown point.
