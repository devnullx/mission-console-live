ONE CAD PER EXPERIMENT

Fifteen payload modules, each packed into a LELP-1 bay

Every experiment's module sheet in the catalog, drawn part by part: the cassette on its microscope stage, the reagent bags, the pumps, the cryo cassette, the furnace, the seed plates, the photobioreactors. Each module is packed into the large bay and checked: does it fit, does it weigh what the catalog says, where is its centre of mass. 15 of 15 fit.

What the modules say

  • Volume is not the limit, mass is. The parts' boxes fill 15 to 55 % of the 21.1 L inside a large bay, and every module sits at or near its 6 kg class limit.
  • The housings are thin. The catalog gives 0.25 to 1.61 kg for structure and containment; as aluminium boxes that is 0.12 to 0.76 mm skins. A 1 mm aluminium box this size weighs about 1.3 kg on its own, so either each module budget carries about 1.5 kg of structure, or the bay's rails carry the launch loads and the module is a light canister. A decision for the team.
  • The microscope sets the depth. The XY stage travels 96.5 x 57.9 mm, so every one of the 24 wells reaches the objective; with the stage the imaging stack is 232 x 151 mm, which sets the module's depth budget.
  • Tightest fits: Bone cells: resorption and mineralization (11 mm spare), Seed germination and root growth (21 mm spare), Immune T-cell activation (28 mm spare), 3D tumour spheroids for drug testing (29 mm spare). Modules with a -80 C cryo cassette and eight or more reagents are the full ones.
  • The waste bag shares its room with the reagent bags: it lies under them and fills as they empty, the way flight fluid packs do. Without that, the waste of a 30-day culture would need a deck of its own.

How they are drawn

Envelope. The twin's large bay module: 313 mm wide, 191 mm deep, 365 mm tall (web/static/scene.js at 11.5 units per metre). Layout. The back face is the bay interface (connector, 10 mm cold-wall band); a 20 mm channel behind the front panel carries the fluid lines; inside, decks on 2 mm shelves with 8 mm corner posts, 4 mm between parts and 6 mm over each deck for the lines. Parts. Standard parts keep their formats: SBS 127.76 x 85.48 mm cassette with 24 wells at 19.3 mm pitch, 120 mm square seed plates, 75 x 25 mm slide-format organ-chips, NEMA 17 stepper. The XY stage travels 96.5 x 57.9 mm so every well reaches the objective. Other parts take their size from their catalog mass: cassettes 1.6 kg/L, electronics 1.1 kg/L, sensors 1.2 kg/L, the cryo cassette 1.0 kg/L; bags hold 75 % of their item's mass as liquid, up to 22 mm thick, with the volumes the catalog states used as stated. Packing. A grid packer places each part, trying every deck choice and fluid-pack shape and keeping the lowest stack. Checks. Every component inside the envelope, no two components' boxes overlapping, parts' masses adding up to the catalog's module mass, the stack within the bay's height. Centre of mass and inertia come from the solids. All of it is in each module's JSON.
